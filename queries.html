Common Patient Queries

Take a look at questions other patients have asked, organized by category.

Appointments

How do I reschedule my appointment?

You can reschedule by calling our front desk or using the contact form on our website at least 24 hours in advance.

General

Do you treat children?

Yes, our pediatric dentist specializes in gentle, friendly care designed specifically for young patients.

Treatment

Is teeth whitening safe?

When performed by a professional using approved methods, teeth whitening is considered safe for most patients.

Billing

Do you accept insurance?

We accept a range of insurance providers. Please contact our front desk to confirm coverage before your visit.

Treatment

How painful is a root canal treatment?

Modern root canal treatment is performed under local anesthesia and is generally no more uncomfortable than a filling.

Appointments

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, any previous dental records if available, and your insurance card if applicable.

General

How often should I get a dental checkup?

We recommend a routine checkup and cleaning every six months for most patients.
